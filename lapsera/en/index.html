Lapsera

Turn the hours you put in into a single timelapse.

About the App

Lapsera is an Android app that photographs your work at regular intervals and turns the result into a single timelapse video when you finish. You just leave your phone propped up, so recording never interrupts what you are doing.

⏱ Set it and forget it

The capture interval adapts to how long you work, so both short and long sessions come out at a watchable length.

🙈 Faces hidden automatically

Every face in the frame is detected and covered with a mosaic or a sticker. You can also blur the background and reveal only your hands.

🎨 Finish it your way

Pick the length, smoothness and quality of the video, then add a colour filter and background music before you export.

📱 Works offline

Capture and rendering both happen on your phone, so you can keep recording even with no signal.
