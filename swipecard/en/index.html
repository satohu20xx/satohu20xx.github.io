SwipeCard

Flashcards you
swipe to learn

SwipeCard is a flashcard app. Flip a card, then swipe right if you knew it and left if you didn't. Cards you didn't know keep coming back until you do. It isn't just for vocabulary: any pair of a question and an answer can be a card.

Get it on Google Play

01

Flip it, then swipe

Tap a card to reveal the answer. Swipe right if you knew it, left if you didn't. Cards you missed come back based on the forgetting curve, and when no more cards appear, you're done.

02

Build your own decks

Import a CSV file with the question in the first column and the answer in the second. A sheet made in Google Sheets can be sent straight to the app from its share menu. You can also add and edit cards inside the app.

As your decks grow, tag them and filter by tag.

03

See your progress at a glance

Each deck shows how many cards are not studied, studying and studied. Progress is kept separately for Q → A, A → Q and both ways, so you can practice in both directions.

04

Check yourself with a test

Take a test of 30, 50 or 100 questions drawn from the cards you've learned, and share your score if you like.

05

Small things that keep you going

Set a daily goal, and a reminder lets you know when you haven't reached it yet. Questions can be read aloud automatically, or you can have a whole deck read to you, with the language chosen per deck. Dark mode is supported too.

Your decks and study records stay on your device and are never sent to a server.
